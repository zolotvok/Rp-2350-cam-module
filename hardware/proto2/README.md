# proto2

Second prototype of the camera module, work in progress. It is built on [proto1](../proto1) and changes how the camera is attached and what the board can talk to.

## Planned changes

The schematic has a section for each of these:

- **Camera supply and clock on the board.** The support circuitry of the [Adafruit OV5640 breakout](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-OV5640-Camera-Breakout-PCB) is moved onto the board: a 24-pin OV5640 module connector (X1), 2.8 V and 1.5 V regulators (TLV74328, TLV74315) and a 24 MHz oscillator. A solder jumper (SJ1) selects the camera clock: generated by the RP2350 on GPIO8 (external XCLK, as on proto1) or taken from the oscillator (internal XCLK). SJ1 is bridged to the RP2350 clock by default and the oscillator (OSC1) is not populated; to switch, fit OSC1, cut the 1–2 bridge and close 2–3. Only the circuit comes from the breakout's schematic; the layout is not copied (see [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md)).
- **Camera wired directly to the RP2350B.** The header pair between the board and the breakout (J2 on the board, JP1/JP2 on the breakout) is removed and the camera signals go straight to the same GPIOs as on proto1, with two differences:
  - D2–D9 are now on GPIO0–GPIO7 in order. The pairwise swap of proto1 is fixed, so the firmware must not reorder the bits on proto2.
  - The camera reset has its own net (CAM_~{RESET}, GPIO22). In the imported breakout circuit it shared the `~{RESET}` net name with the RP2350 RUN pin.
- **CAN bus.** An MCP2515 CAN controller and an SN65HVD230 transceiver.
- **I²C pull-ups and level shifting.**
- **Additional LEDs.**

## Status

Snapshot from October 2026:

- **Schematic:** the new blocks are added; wiring them to the RP2350B is in progress.
- **PCB:** the layout has not been updated yet and is still the proto1 layout.
